Traditional Chicken Kabsa

Prep Time: 20 mins | Cook Time: 50 mins

Kabsa is the ultimate comfort food, bringing families together over a single, beautifully spiced platter of rice and meat.

Ingredients:

1 kg Chicken, 2 cups Basmati rice, 2 chopped onions, 3 tomatoes (pureed), Kabsa spice mix (cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, loomi), raisins, and toasted almonds for garnish.

Instructions:

1. Sauté onions and garlic in olive oil, then add the chicken and Kabsa spices until browned.
2. Pour in the tomato puree and water, then let the chicken simmer until fully cooked.
3. Remove the chicken to roast in the oven. Use the remaining broth to cook the basmati rice.
4. Serve the rice on a large platter, place the golden chicken on top, and garnish with raisins and almonds.

Classic Spaghetti Pomodoro

Prep Time: 10 mins | Cook Time: 15 mins

The secret to this timeless Italian dish lies in the quality of the olive oil and using fresh, fragrant basil leaves.

Ingredients:

200g Spaghetti, 4 fresh tomatoes, 3 cloves of garlic (sliced), 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, fresh basil leaves, salt, and grated Parmesan cheese.

Instructions:

1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water until it is al dente.
2. In a pan, gently heat olive oil and sauté the sliced garlic until fragrant but not brown.
3. Add crushed fresh tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens.
4. Toss the cooked pasta directly into the sauce, add fresh basil, and top with a generous dusting of Parmesan cheese.

Berry Fluffy Pancakes

Prep Time: 10 mins | Cook Time: 10 mins

Achieving the perfect fluffiness requires mixing the batter gently—small lumps are actually a good thing!

Ingredients:

1 cup all-purpose flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp salt, 1 cup milk, 1 egg, 2 tbsp melted butter, fresh blueberries, and maple syrup.

Instructions:

1. Whisk dry ingredients in one bowl and wet ingredients in another. Combine them gently.
2. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and grease lightly with butter.
3. Pour batter onto the skillet. Drop a few fresh blueberries onto each pancake.
4. Flip when bubbles form on the surface, cook until golden on both sides, and serve with warm maple syrup.
